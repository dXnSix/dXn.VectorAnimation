# Rive files

Files downloaded from the [Rive community](https://rive.app/community/), named by their community ids and titles.
Played by the gallery app, compared against Rive's own runtime while the importer is built, and shown on the
documentation site's Examples page (fetched from beside the examples app, `rive-files`). Their authors are not
recorded in the downloads.

The files were added on the repository owner's own responsibility, under the terms the Rive community offers them
with.
